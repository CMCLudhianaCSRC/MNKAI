System

Built like a research instrument.

PanDerm-L, frozenDermatology foundation model (ViT-L/16) pretrained on 2.1 M images without labels · features from three depths · crop + whole-image views.
One model, many conditionsFive-head ensemble for clinical photos and dermoscopy · separate cancer check tuned on dark skin (DDI).
Leak-proof validationNear-duplicates, patients and lesions kept in one fold · every choice made out-of-fold under pre-declared rules.
Calibrated & conformalTemperature scaling · 90% possible-diagnoses list · single answer only when that confidence was right 90% of the time.
